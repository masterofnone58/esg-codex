Multi-tenant ESG SaaS MVP

TraceLight ESG Cloud

Collect store evidence, log logistics data, generate compliance-ready ESG reports, and let executives drill into supply-chain impact.

Operating model

One platform, three working views

Store managers capture evidence on mobile. ESG admins validate inputs, manage logistics data, and publish reports. Executives track the supply chain and inspect carbon, evidence, and supplier credentials.

MVP pillars

  • Responsive browser portal for desktop and mobile
  • Tenant-specific dashboards and access boundaries
  • Evidence capture with photos, material type, and weight
  • Admin reporting workspace for compliance output
  • Executive drill-down into suppliers and certificates
